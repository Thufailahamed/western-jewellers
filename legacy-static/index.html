The Craft

Four hands.
Forty hours.
One ring.

01/04
  1. 01

    Drawn

    Every commission starts as a pencil sketch at the bench, drawn with you and changed until it is right.

  2. 02

    Cast

    Recycled gold and platinum, cast in-house and then hand-forged so the metal is dense and strong.

  3. 03

    Set

    Each stone is set under the microscope. Claws are shaped one by one to hold it tightly and let in as much light as possible.

  4. 04

    Finished

    Seven stages of polishing, then a final check under daylight lamps. Then it is boxed and ready for you.
